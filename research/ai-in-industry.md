## 2. Five ideas for 247 one-of-a-kind pens

1. **Grounded natural-language catalog search** (Etsy/eBay): "flexible 14k nib, lever filler, under $200" → in-stock SKUs; 1stDibs cut null results 25%.
2. **Photo-first buy/consign intake** (Chrono24, TheRealReal Shield): seller photos → likely maker/model/era plus checklist, routed to Nathaniel; no price given.
3. **Blog-as-knowledge-base Q&A** (Klarna): 261 posts, the starter series and fake-Montblanc series are the only answer source, with citations.
4. **Nib-width translator**: vintage/Western/Japanese widths → mm and "what it writes like."
5. **Structured condition disclosure** (StockX/TheRealReal "what was checked"): his restoration notes as a consistent checklist.

## 3. Three unsolved pain points

1. **Condition uncertainty and misdescription.** FPN: "Parts can be more expensive than the pen itself"; consensus is "buy from a reputable dealer who will have restored the pen properly" ([FPN](https://www.fountainpennetwork.com/forum/topic/351556-advice-for-buying-vintage-fountain-pens/)). Pen Addict: "slight damage" was super-glued rings, "micro-scratches" were deep, refunds refused ([Pen Addict](https://www.penaddict.com/blog/2015/8/28/buyers-remorse-impulse-buys-and-shady-sellers-the-vagaries-of-buying-fountain-pens-online)).
2. **Fakes, especially Montblanc.** eBay fakes are "numerous enough that reporting them all is difficult"; sellers often don't know ([FPN](https://www.fountainpennetwork.com/forum/topic/357889-on-fake-montblanc-pens-at-ebay/)).
3. **Where to start, including nib widths.** Don't buy first vintage pens on eBay "unless you're prepared to do a ton of research" ([FPN](http://www.fountainpennetwork.com/forum/topic/155807-first-foray-into-vintage-pens/)); Western F ≈ Japanese M, varies by brand ([Goulet](https://www.gouletpens.com/blogs/fountain-pen-blog/japanese-vs-western-fountain-pen-nibs)). No r/fountainpens threads surfaced **[unverified]**.

## 4. Guardrails

- Prices, availability, condition only from the live catalog record; never a model-generated price.
- No authenticity claim beyond what Nathaniel wrote on that listing.
- Sell-in requests: collect photos and details, never estimate value; hand off to owner.
- Disclose AI on first message; offer human handoff at any point.
- FTC: no AI overclaims ([Keep your AI claims in check](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/blog/2023/02/keep-your-ai-claims-check); [Operation AI Comply](https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2024/09/ftc-announces-crackdown-deceptive-ai-claims-schemes)); no AI or fake reviews, penalties up to $51,744 per violation ([Fake Reviews Rule, eff. 2024-10-21](https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2024/08/federal-trade-commission-announces-final-rule-banning-fake-reviews-testimonials)).
- Returns: quote only the posted policy; Connecticut requires it displayed on the site and honored ([§ 42-110aa](https://law.justia.com/codes/connecticut/title-42/chapter-735c/section-42-110aa)). Ship within stated time or 30 days, else consent-to-delay or refund ([FTC Mail/Internet Order Rule](https://www.ftc.gov/legal-library/browse/rules/mail-internet-or-telephone-order-merchandise-rule)).
- No repair-outcome or turnaround promises unless the owner stated them.
- Log every chat; escalate pens over $1,000 to the owner.
