Google reviews

Reviews live on your Google Business Profile, which is free to set up at business.google.com. Once you have one, paste its Place ID here (find it with Google's “Place ID Finder”). From then on every page carries a “Review us on Google” link that opens the review box for your shop, which is the fastest way to get your first reviews.
