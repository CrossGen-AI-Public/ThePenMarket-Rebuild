Every pen on this site passed through my hands.

Owner Nathaniel Cerf has been a fountain pen junkie since the age of 9, when he first found his late grandfather’s Sheaffer Lifetime Balance and began writing with it. Years later, while teaching a fencing class in Sioux Falls, South Dakota, Cerf met a fountain pen repairman who began teaching him the art and science of vintage pen repair.

  • Restored: lever-fillers, button-fillers, crescent-fillers, Vacumatics, TouchDowns and Sheaffer Snorkels
  • Described honestly, capped length and flaws included
  • Return it within 14 days for a full refund of the item price
  • Problems found within 30 days: “let us know and we’ll do what we can to fix it”
